## OVERVIEW
STRANDED is a survival game where the player must attempt to survive 50 nights on a deserted island after a plane crash. The player must explore the island to gather materials for food, shelter, and weapons to survive and defend themselves from entities on the island. The island is almost utopian during the day to the point where something seems eerie.

## FEATURES
1) This game is set in a 3D world on a deserted island and should be an open world game that the players can explore. As the player levels up the campfire, more of the map should be unlocked at each level. Start the biome in the forest and unlock the desert biome at level 50 of the campfire, though the desert doesn't have much helpful materials. There should be a slight horror element to the game where it seems utopian during the day where the player almost expects there to be something wrong.

2) Players have a hunger and health bar. Hunger drops over time, decreasing faster when the player sprints, whereas health only decreases when the player takes damage from monsters or entities. 

3) The player should be able to chop down trees, and kill livestock for raw meat and materials like leather, wool or feathers. Dropping the raw meat in the fire cooks it and allows the player to reduce their hunger more. 

4) Players can tame wolves as pets by feeding them raw meat which help them hunt during the day, bringing back food to the campfire, and also help fight off entities and monsters at night.

5) The open world map has 3 major boss locations the player can seek out and defeat for rarer weapon and item drops. The bosses are the tree spirit which drops shoes that allow the player to sprint faster without taking up stamina/hunger and healing staff, a cult camp of warriors which one defeated drops an enchanted spear that deals the most damage out of all weapons, and a giant spider and a spider army nest that once defeated provides the player with the best armor in the game.

6) If the player unlocks the desert biome, there is a snake king boss and snake army the player can defeat to gain venom abilities which they can use on entities and monsters. 

7) Players have a crafting table where they can craft different weapons from spears to guns using wood and metal ore that they must chop or mine from the forest.

8) Random chests with loot, weapons, or other survival items like bandages and medkits are placed randomly throughout the forest.

9) This game should have sound as the player walks or does different actions and forest sounds as background noise. Monsters and entities should make a specific sound to help alert the player of their presence nearby.

## RULES
1) The player must keep the campfire lit at all times. If it goes out for more than 10 seconds the safe zone around the campfire disappears and entities will begin swarming the area even during the day. The campfire can be leveled up once the max number of logs for that level is reached. This should increment as levels go up.
2) Entities and monsters will attack the campfire every other night and the player must defend themselves with their weapons. Every 10 days, the blood moon occurs and a boss will attack the campfire which the player must defend themselves from. The difficulty of defending from monsters at night should increase every 3 days. 
3) A merchant will come through every other day and the player can complete the task required by the merchant to earn items they are selling that day.
4) After Night 50, a plane will come pick the player up and they officially win the game. Players can attempt the game multiple times and their highest streak of days is tracked. 

## USER CONTROLS
Use Up arrow or W key and Down arrow or S key to move forward and backward, respectively. Use A and D keys or Left and Right arrows to change perspective. Players can also use their mouse to change their perspective. 

Shift to Sprint

Click to eat, use an item, or complete actions like chopping down trees or attacking a monster.

Q to drop an item, E to pick it up.

The player should be able to navigate through their inventory.

## SCREENS
The first time the player plays this game, they should have a tutorial screen that leads them through a series of basic tasks that teach them all the different functions they need to know about the game.

There should be a home screen where the player can pause the game to get to, adjust sound settings, adjust control settings like sensitivity, or view their game history.

## IMPLEMENTATION
Use TypeScript and Three.js for the 3D rendering. For the end product, I want to be able to deploy the game on Github Pages.


## Priorities
The player should be able to complete the basic functions first. Sound, boss locations, rare loot drops, and desert biome should left for last. Collecting materials, crafting and using weapons, hunger, health, and monsters are part of basic functions.
