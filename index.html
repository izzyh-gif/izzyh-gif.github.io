About me

A little about who I am and what I'm curious about.

Hi! I'm Isabella, a sophomore in Information Systems student at CMU exploring how AI and technology can transform organizations from business, healthcare, and user-centered perspectives.

I recently started research at the Human Computer Interaction Institute in the Data Interaction Group (DIG).
