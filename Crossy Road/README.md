Game Introduction:
This game is Cowsy Road, a similar game to Crossy Road, except instead of a chicken you have a cow. To make the game more fun, jetpack power-ups were added where the cow gets launched several lanes forward every time you collect a jet-pack.

How to Play:
Movement - Use the arrow keys to move forward, backward, left and right. 
Losing - Avoid getting run over by cars or jumping into the river and sinking (while some cows can swim for short distances, this cow unfortunately cannot). Moving off-screen, whether on a log or using your controls, will also cause game over. 
Scoring - Each lane you move forward on adds 1 point to your score. 
Jetpacks/Obstacles - Jetpacks will always launch you into a grass lane. You can move in any direction to collect jetpacks, but you can't walk into trees or bushes. 

AI Models/Tools:
This game was coded using Kiro AI, specifically with the Claude Sonnet 4.6 model.

Tip/Added difficulty due to reaching time limit: Sometimes you won't have anywhere to go (like another log to jump on for the next river lane), so be sure to anticipate and plan out your path! It might serve you better to wait until you see a clear path to move forward.
